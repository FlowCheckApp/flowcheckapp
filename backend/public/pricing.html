What happens after the 7-day trial?

Your selected subscription begins unless you cancel before the trial ends. The current options are $34.99 per year or $4.99 per month.

How is payment handled?

Payment is processed by Apple through your App Store account. FlowCheck does not receive or store your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings under Apple ID → Subscriptions. Access continues through the end of the current billing period.

How are refunds handled?

App Store purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple. You can begin a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.
