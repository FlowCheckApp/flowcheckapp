01

Bank connection

How do I connect my bank?

In FlowCheck, open Settings or Connected Banks and choose Add Bank. Plaid Link will guide you through selecting and authenticating with your institution.

Why is my bank not syncing?

Some institutions require you to reconnect after changing a password or security setting. Open Connected Banks and follow any reconnect prompt. If the problem continues, email support with your institution name only.

Can FlowCheck move my money?

No. Bank access is read-only. FlowCheck uses approved balance and transaction data to calculate your experience; it cannot initiate transfers or purchases.

02

Account & security

How do I turn on Face ID?

Open FlowCheck Settings, choose Security, and enable Face ID. iOS will ask for permission the first time.

How do I reset my password?

From the FlowCheck sign-in screen, choose Forgot Password and enter the email on your account. Follow the reset link sent to that address.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. Deleting your FlowCheck account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that separately in iPhone Settings.

03

Trial & billing

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Your selected Apple subscription begins unless you cancel before the trial ends. Current pricing is $34.99 per year or $4.99 per month.

How do I cancel?

On iPhone, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → FlowCheck, then choose Cancel Subscription. Billing is managed entirely by Apple.

I paid, but the app is still locked.

Open the FlowCheck paywall and choose Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple ID used to subscribe. If access does not return, email support with the approximate purchase date—never your Apple password.

04

Privacy & data

Does FlowCheck sell my financial data?

No. FlowCheck does not sell financial data and does not run an advertising business.

How do I request a copy of my data?

Email privacy@getflowcheck.app from the address associated with your account and use the subject “Data Access Request.”
